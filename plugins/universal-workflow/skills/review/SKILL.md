# Review

1. Identify the exact deliverable or version, the governing brief, and the standards that apply. For changes to existing work, establish the relevant baseline. Use the intended audience and use case to interpret the requirements. State which checks are limited by missing inputs or access.
2. Assess two dimensions independently. **Fit to the brief** covers completeness, fidelity to confirmed decisions, and behavior in representative scenarios. **Fitness for use** covers correctness, usability, and applicable standards. Inspect the actual result and independently verify the evidence behind consequential claims. When a fresh reviewer is available and delegation is permitted, give them the brief, standards, and deliverable before sharing the implementer's conclusions.
3. Report material findings in severity order. For each, give the affected location, the requirement or standard, the observed evidence, the consequence, and what a correction needs to achieve. State the readiness verdict as ready, changes needed, or verification incomplete. Distinguish gaps in verification from observed defects.

End with the review report. Apply fixes when separately requested. When reviewing a revised deliverable, recheck the findings and affected requirements.
